Privacy policy: CapyPets: Emoji Battery

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Who is responsible

CapyPets: Emoji Battery ("the app") is published by ArqonHQ, Germany. Privacy contact: playarqon@gmail.com.

Summary

  • No account is needed. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Preferences, settings and usage rights are stored on your phone. When analytics is enabled, some style identifiers and actions are also included in usage events.
  • The Accessibility service draws a customization strip with time, connection status, battery and decorations, and an optional reaction when connecting a charger. It does not read your screen, your touches, messages or passwords, and it does not perform actions for you.
  • The app shows Google AdMob ads and uses Google Firebase to measure usage. In the EU, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's privacy message before data is used for personalised ads or analytics.

Data that stays on your phone

The app keeps the following in your phone's private storage: chosen styles, settings, favourites, saved looks, unlocked styles and when they expire, and your Premium status. We do not send it to any server of ours: we have none. Android backup is disabled for this data. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

  • Bluetooth (optional, Android 12+): only to know whether Bluetooth is on and show it in the bar. It does not scan for or read devices.
  • Notifications (optional, Android 13+): only to remind you 30 minutes before a 24-hour unlocked style expires.
  • Internet and network state: to load ads, analytics, remote configuration and Google Play purchases.
