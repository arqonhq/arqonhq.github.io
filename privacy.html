This policy covers the Android apps published by ArqonHQ: 4 Emojis 1 Película, Emoji Battery, Emoji Bible Quiz and Impostor Game (the "apps"). The first part applies to every app. The table below lists the third-party services each app uses, and some apps have an extra section with details that only apply to them.

1. Who is responsible

ArqonHQ (Germany) is the data controller. Contact for any privacy question or request: playarqon@gmail.com. We answer within one month.

2. What we collect

  • No accounts. The apps do not ask for your name, email, phone number or password.
  • Stored on your device only. Game progress, settings and any nickname you type are saved on your phone and never sent to our servers. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Collected by third-party services. The services listed in section 3 may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID or an app instance ID), your IP address (used to infer approximate country or city, never precise location), device model, operating system, app version, how you use the app, and crash and diagnostic reports.
  • The apps do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, messages, microphone or camera data.

4. Why we use this data (legal basis)

  • Running the app and fixing crashes (Crashlytics, purchases): our legitimate interest in providing a working app, and the contract when you buy something.
  • Ads (AdMob): consent where the law requires it. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later from the app.
  • Analytics (Firebase): our legitimate interest in improving the app, limited to usage statistics and device information. PostHog is used only if you turn it on.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone other than the services in section 3, which process it to provide ads, analytics, crash reports and purchases. We may disclose data if required by law.

6. Retention

Data collected by the services in section 3 is kept according to their own policies, and for no longer than 24 months for analytics data. Data on your device is kept until you uninstall the app.

7. International transfers

Some services may process data outside the European Economic Area. In that case they rely on safeguards approved under the GDPR, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13. Apps that ask for your age do not show ads and do not enable optional analytics for users who say they are under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to its processing, receive a copy, and withdraw consent at any time. Write to playarqon@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (list of EU authorities). California residents have equivalent rights to know, delete and opt out under the CCPA/CPRA, using the same email.

You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings, and uninstall the app at any time to stop all collection.

10. 4 Emojis 1 Película

  • Ads: only non-personalized ads with content suitable for all ages. No ads are shown to users who say they are under 13 or who have purchased an ad-free option. You can review your ad consent from the in-app settings when the law requires this option.
  • Firebase: gameplay events such as levels started, completed or failed, hints used and ads watched, plus device information and crash reports.
  • PostHog (optional): after onboarding the app asks whether you want to share detailed usage statistics. It stays off unless you accept, it is never enabled for users under 13, and you can turn it off at any time in the app settings. Data is stored on PostHog's EU servers.
  • Purchases: RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase status so purchases can be restored. Payments are processed by Google Play; we never see your card details.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification token is sent to us.

11. Emoji Battery and the Accessibility service

Full details are in the Emoji Battery (CapyPets) privacy policy. In short: Emoji Battery uses Android's AccessibilityService API only to draw the emoji battery indicator over the status bar. It does not read, collect, store or share what is on your screen, what you type, or any personal data through this permission. The service is off until you turn it on in Android settings, and you can turn it off at any time.

12. Changes

We will post any changes on this page with a new date.
