Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy covers the Android apps published by ArqonHQ (contact: playarqon@gmail.com): Emoji Battery, 4 Emojis 1 Película, Emoji Bible Quiz and Impostor Game.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or account. The apps use these third-party services, which may collect device data on their own:

  • Google AdMob (ads): advertising ID, IP address, device and app interaction data, used to show and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first (Google's consent message), and you can change your choice in the app.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Remote Config, Crashlytics if used): app usage events, device model, OS version, approximate location from IP and crash reports, used to improve the app.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

Emoji Battery and the Accessibility service

Emoji Battery uses Android's AccessibilityService API only to draw the emoji battery indicator over the status bar. It does not read, collect, store or share what is on your screen, what you type, or any personal data through this permission. The service is off until you turn it on in Android settings, and you can turn it off at any time.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your data. Data is only processed by the services listed above to provide ads, analytics and crash reports.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13.

Your rights (GDPR)

You can ask for access, correction or deletion of data, or object to processing, by writing to playarqon@gmail.com. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings, and withdraw ad consent inside the app.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page with a new date.
